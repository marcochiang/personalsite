The Us Agreement ♥

We don't need to define ourselves by what a relationship is supposed to look like. We have something beautiful, unconventional and uniquely ours. We want to discover what brings each of us joy, comfort, connection, freedom and fulfillment.

Love is freely given. Attraction is never owed.
Everything is an invitation, not an obligation.

Our Five Promises

  1. 1 Love is not a transaction. Kindness, generosity, emotional support, gifts and experiences are freely offered. None of these creates an obligation for romance, sex or anything in return.
  2. 2 We honor attraction as it exists. We don't need to force chemistry, and neither person has to apologize for not feeling something.
  3. 3 Curiosity over expectations. We're free to explore different forms of connection, including touch and intimacy, only when both of us genuinely want to.
  4. 4 Freedom and honesty coexist. We can develop meaningful connections with other people while being thoughtful and transparent about our own boundaries.
  5. 5 We can always change our minds. A Yes today isn't a Yes forever. A Maybe isn't a promise. A No deserves respect without debate.

Signed with love, freedom and curiosity, Marco ♥ Kalypso

This is a living agreement. We can revisit it whenever our feelings or circumstances change.
